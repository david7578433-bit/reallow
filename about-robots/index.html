RealLowBot

RealLowBot is the price robot behind RealLow, a free price-comparison site for shoppers.

  • It identifies itself honestly: RealLowBot/1.0 (+https://reallow.sites.tab.bot/about-robots; price comparison for shoppers).
  • It reads robots.txt first and skips anything it disallows.
  • It runs every 4 hours and waits 1.5 seconds or more between requests to the same site.
  • It stops right away when a site blocks it (bot check, 403, 429 or 503) and does not try to get around it.
  • It only reads public prices and links back to the store's own product page.

Store owners: to ask us to stop or to fix a price, open an issue on GitHub or block RealLowBot in your robots.txt.
